# Domain model and data dictionary

**Status:** W0-04 planning baseline  
**Source of truth:** [Legacy Real Estate Agency SRS](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1AbwUbZ9NO1AL77SWdsERqQkgy_xw2Z57k8unsJp--bI/edit), especially sections 1–2, 6, 10, 14, and 15.

This dictionary records the entities, key fields, and relationships named in
SRS section 15. The SRS says the developer owns the final schema but should
keep these entity names and relationships. This is a requirements reference,
not a database schema or a decision to implement every listed entity in V1.
Where the SRS does not define a convention, that gap is identified rather
than filled with an assumed rule.

## Entities and relationships

| SRS entity name | Key fields named by the SRS | Relationships named by the SRS |
|---|---|---|
| Branch | name, town, paybill numbers | Has many Properties, Users, LandProjects. |
| User | name, phone, email, roles, branches, 2FA | Creates and approves records. |
| Landlord | name, ID/reg no., KRA PIN, payout details | Has many Properties, ManagementAgreements, Remittances. |
| ManagementAgreement | commission model, fees, payout day, MRI flag, dates | Belongs to Landlord and Property. |
| Property | name, type, location, GPS, amenities | Belongs to Branch; has many Units, Caretakers, Expenses. |
| Unit | code, type, bedrooms, status, meters, listing flag | Belongs to Property; has many Tenancies, RentRates. |
| RentRate / UnitCharge | amount, charge type, effective from/to | Belongs to Unit. |
| Tenant | name, ID, KRA PIN, phone, email, type | Has many Tenancies. |
| Tenancy | start, end, rent, deposit, due day, notice, status | Links Tenant and Unit; has many Invoices, Payments, DepositEntries. |
| Invoice / InvoiceLine | number, period, lines, total, status | Belongs to a Tenancy or PlotSale. |
| Payment | channel, M-Pesa/bank ref, amount, payer phone, status | Allocated to Invoices via PaymentAllocation. |
| PaymentAllocation | Named as the allocation relationship; no key fields specified. | Links Payments to Invoices. |
| MpesaTransaction | raw callback payload, unique trans ID | Becomes a Payment once matched. |
| MeterReading | unit, type, previous, current, photo, date | Creates InvoiceLines. |
| MaintenanceTicket | category, priority, status, cost, vendor | Belongs to Unit or Property. |
| Vendor | name, trade, phone, M-Pesa | Has many Tickets, Expenses. |
| Expense | category, amount, property, approval status | Charged to Landlord or Agency. |
| Remittance | period, gross, commission, expenses, tax, net, status | Belongs to Landlord. |
| LandProject / Plot | title nos., size, price, status | A LandProject has many Plots. |
| Buyer | name, ID, KRA PIN, phone, KYC docs | Has many PlotSales. |
| PlotSale | price, deposit, plan, schedule, status | Links Buyer and Plot; has many Instalments, TransferSteps. |
| Instalment | Named as a PlotSale child; no key fields specified in section 15. | Belongs to a PlotSale. |
| TransferStep | Named as a PlotSale child; no key fields specified in section 15. | Belongs to a PlotSale. |
| Lead / Viewing | source, needs, agent, status | Links to Unit or Plot. |
| Message | channel, template, recipient, status | Linked to any party. |
| Document | type, file, uploaded by | Attached to any record. |
| JournalEntry / JournalLine | date, account, debit, credit, source | Generated by financial events; the SRS does not otherwise detail their relationship. |
| FailedJob | queue, job type, payload, error, attempts, failed at | Reviewed and retried from the admin screen. |
| AuditLog | user, action, entity, old/new values, IP, time | No additional relationship is specified in the SRS table. |

Paired names such as `Invoice / InvoiceLine` are both SRS entity names. The
relationship descriptions above preserve the SRS's stated links; they do not
add cardinalities, keys, or constraints beyond what it says.

## Conventions supported by the SRS

### Names and relationships

- Preserve the entity names and relationships in the table above (SRS
  section 15).
- The SRS does not prescribe database table casing, ORM class names, primary
  key format, or a general foreign-key naming pattern. Those choices remain
  open for the schema-design task; this document does not impose them.
- `PaymentAllocation` is named as the link between payments and invoices.
  The SRS does not define its key fields or allocation algorithm here.

### Branch scope

- The business has Nairobi and Nakuru branches. The SRS states that every
  record belongs to a branch and staff see only their branch unless granted
  otherwise (sections 1.1 and 2).
- Branch-scoped access must be enforced server-side. A cross-branch grant is
  possible; its roles and permission details are configurable and are not
  further defined by this dictionary.
- Section 15 explicitly relates Branch to Properties, Users, and
  LandProjects. For other records, the exact direct-versus-related branch
  ownership mechanism is not specified. The final schema must preserve
  branch ownership and isolation without inventing a different scope model.
- The tenant blacklist is visible across branches in section 5; apply that
  stated exception through authorized access rather than treating all
  tenant information as globally visible.

### Money and currency

- The SRS specifies KES display with thousands separators (section 14.4),
  and says rent, deposits, charges, payments, expenses, and remittances must
  be traceable (sections 6 and 10).
- Section 10 describes separate agency-income, landlord-trust, and
  deposit-trust pots. The SRS also discusses a double-entry journal, while
  the approved W0-01 baseline records the full V1 accounting boundary as
  unresolved. Do not treat this dictionary as approval to implement a
  general ledger or choose a V1 accounting policy.
- The SRS does not specify numeric storage precision, scale, rounding rules,
  or minor-unit handling. These must be agreed with the finance owner before
  a database representation or calculation policy is selected. Do not use
  this document to infer those values.

### Dates, times, and phone numbers

- Display dates as `DD/MM/YYYY` and use the `Africa/Nairobi` time zone
  (`EAT`) for the user-facing system (SRS section 14.4).
- Store Kenyan phone numbers in the international formats shown by the SRS:
  `2547XXXXXXXX` or `2541XXXXXXXX`, and validate them.
- The SRS does not prescribe database timestamp storage, UTC conversion,
  timestamp precision, or whether each named date is a date-only value or a
  time-bearing value. Those are schema decisions, not settled here.

### Statuses

- Status is a field in several SRS entities, but the SRS does not provide a
  universal status vocabulary. Keep status rules specific to each workflow;
  do not introduce a shared enum by assumption.
- Explicit examples/flows in the SRS include:
  - Unit listing eligibility uses `Vacant` and `Notice Given` (section 11).
  - Lead status uses `new`, `contacted`, `viewing booked`, `viewed`,
    `applied`, `won`, and `lost` with a reason (section 11.2).
  - Maintenance tickets follow `Open → Assigned → Quotation → Awaiting
    landlord approval (if above threshold) → In progress → Completed →
    Closed (tenant confirms)` (section 8.1).
  - Plot status uses `available`, `reserved`, `sold – paying`, `sold –
    fully paid`, `title processing`, `title issued`, and `cancelled`
    (section 7.1).
- The SRS does not define the complete status sets or transition rules for
  every entity. Do not infer them from these examples.

### Audit and financial record history

- The SRS requires an audit log users cannot edit and identifies user,
  action, entity, old/new values, IP, and time (sections 2, 14.1, and 15).
- Audit coverage includes creates, edits, approvals, and logins (section 2).
  The SRS does not specify an event schema, retention exception, or
  implementation mechanism here.
- Financial records are reversed rather than hard-deleted (sections 2 and
  14). The specific reversal flows remain part of the relevant finance
  requirements and approvals.

## Scope and unresolved details

- Section 15 covers the overall product model, not just Phase 1. Land sales,
  leads/viewings, and the accounting journal are not automatically V1
  commitments; use the approved [W0-01 scope baseline](./srs/W0-01-scope-baseline.md)
  for phase boundaries.
- The SRS does not fully define IDs, uniqueness constraints, nullability,
  deletion/archival behavior for non-financial records, all entity statuses,
  branch ownership for indirectly related records, or exact field types.
- Before implementing the schema, resolve the money precision and accounting
  boundary with the finance owner and document any other decisions not
  specified by the SRS. Until then, this W0-04 deliverable records the
  requirements only; it does not authorize additional domain behavior.
